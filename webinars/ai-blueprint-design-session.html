California AI Blueprint Design Session

A first look at California's AI Workforce Blueprint — and a working session to help shape it.

~60 minutes Online via Zoom Open to all 45 boards & partners Free
Register free →
A working session, not a lecture

The Design Session is where CWA brings the workforce system together to shape how AI supports the field. Each session shares progress on the Blueprint, previews new tools in development, and learns from what boards are already doing. Come to see what's being built, weigh in on priorities, and help design a Blueprint that meets all 45 boards where they are.

Why attend

What you'll walk away with

1

Progress on the Blueprint

An updated look at where the Blueprint design process stands and what's taking shape.

2

A real say in the design

Shape the work directly — take part through interactive polls and shareouts from the field.

3

Ways to participate

Learn about concrete opportunities to get involved and contribute going forward.

4

Learn from peers

Hear from others across California working through similar challenges.
